About OddZone

Nigeria's Skill-Based Gaming Platform

OddZone lets players sharpen their reflexes, practice for free, and challenge other gamers in competitive 1v1 stake battles.

How It Works:

  • Practice Runs: Play 2-minute timed runs completely free to sharpen your scores and climb the practice leaderboard.
  • 1v1 Stake Challenges: Stake Naira (₦100 – ₦5,000) on your favorite games. Play a 2-minute match and submit your best score.
  • Matchmaking: The platform matches you with an opponent who played at the same stake. The player with the highest score wins the pool (less 10% platform fee).
  • Instant Deposits & Fast Payouts: Deposit seamlessly via Paystack. Withdraw directly to any verified Nigerian bank account.
  • CBN Compliance & KYC: Strict Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN) NUBAN check digit algorithms and BVN/NIN identity resolution protect fair play and prevent fraud.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

At OddZone, your privacy and data security are our top priorities. This Privacy Policy details how we handle your personal and financial information.

1. Information We Collect:

We collect your email address, chosen username, and gameplay scores. For cash withdrawals, we collect your bank name, account number, and legal name verified via BVN/NIN.

2. ID & Banking Security:

All BVN and NIN identifiers are masked and never stored in plain text. Bank lookups are performed directly through licensed CBN-regulated payment partners (Paystack).

3. Data Sharing:

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. Information is strictly used for platform authentication, match settlement, and statutory compliance.

Terms and Conditions

Effective: September 2026

1. Age & Eligibility:

You must be at least 18 years of age to participate in real-money stake challenges or request cash withdrawals.

2. Pure Skill Gaming:

OddZone competitions are 100% games of skill. Game outcomes depend entirely on player hand-eye coordination, timing, and score optimization, not random chance.

3. Stake & Payout Rules:

Stakes are placed into a match pool. The winner receives 90% of the total pool; a 10% platform fee is retained by OddZone. In the event of an exact score tie, stakes are refunded in full.

4. Fair Play & Anti-Cheat:

The use of bots, automated scripts, memory editors, or exploits is strictly forbidden. Accounts suspected of cheating will be permanently banned and associated funds confiscated.

Delete Account?

This action is permanent and cannot be undone. All your game scores, verification details, and local progress will be completely erased.
